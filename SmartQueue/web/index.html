About Us

Transforming Healthcare Queue Management

SmartQueue was built to solve the frustration of long clinic waiting times. We provide clinics and hospitals with a modern digital queue management system that gives patients visibility, reduces stress, and helps staff work more efficiently.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make healthcare more accessible and less stressful by giving patients the information they need and giving clinics the tools to serve better. We believe no patient should have to stand in a long queue not knowing when they will be seen.

Our Vision

A South African healthcare system where every clinic runs efficiently, every patient is respected, and digital tools bridge the gap between patients and providers.

Our Services

Everything Your Clinic Needs

SmartQueue provides a complete suite of tools for clinics of all sizes — from a single GP practice to a large hospital with multiple departments.

Online Appointment Booking

Patients can book appointments 24/7 from any device. Select department, date, time and receive instant confirmation with a queue number.

  • Real-time slot availability
  • SMS confirmation
  • Easy cancellation

Live Queue Management

Staff can call patients, update queue status, and manage walk-ins all from a single dashboard. Patients see live updates on their phones.

  • Priority queue support
  • Walk-in registration
  • Public display screen

Patient Management

Maintain complete digital patient profiles including visit history, appointment records, and contact details — all POPIA compliant.

  • Digital patient profiles
  • Visit history tracking
  • POPIA compliant storage

Reports and Analytics

Admin dashboards with real-time insights into patient volume, wait times, department performance and daily appointment summaries.

  • Daily and weekly reports
  • Department analytics
  • Exportable data

SMS Notifications

Automated SMS alerts keep patients informed at every step — booking confirmation, queue reminders and when their turn is approaching.

  • Booking confirmation SMS
  • Queue position alerts
  • Appointment reminders

Offline Support

SmartQueue works even without internet. Data is cached locally and automatically synced to the server when the connection is restored.

  • Local data caching
  • Auto-sync on reconnect
  • No data loss
